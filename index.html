一、应用简介

SilverMind 银心是一款面向长者的日常生活与认知练习应用，提供生活计算、时间安排、记忆及文化等练习内容。本政策说明应用如何处理使用过程中产生的信息。

二、信息的处理与保存

应用无需注册账号即可使用。练习日期、答题情况、完成记录及用户主动填写的反馈内容，可保存在设备本地，以便展示练习进度和历史记录。根据当前应用网页代码的设计，这些记录不需要上传到开发者服务器才能完成日常练习。

如果用户主动使用备份功能，应用会生成备份文件，由用户自行选择保存和分享的位置；恢复功能会读取用户主动选择的备份文件。请妥善保管备份文件，其中可能包含练习记录或反馈内容。

三、权限、网络与第三方服务

SilverMind 的核心练习功能设计为可在离线状态下使用。应用不要求用户提供姓名、电话号码或注册账号。实际系统权限、可能包含的第三方组件及网络连接行为以正式发布的 APK 为准；开发者应在发布前核查并据实更新本节。

四、数据管理

用户可以通过应用提供的记录管理或恢复功能管理相关记录（如适用）。如需清除保存在设备内的应用数据，可在 Android 系统设置中找到 SilverMind，使用“清除存储空间/清除数据”功能。该操作通常不可恢复，请先自行备份需要保留的记录。卸载应用也可能删除本地记录。

五、儿童与长者使用

本应用主要面向长者及其家人，不以儿童为目标用户。建议用户避免在反馈栏填写身份证号码、联系方式、健康病史等敏感个人信息。

六、政策更新

如应用功能、数据处理方式或适用要求发生变化，本政策可能更新。更新后的版本将在本页面发布，并标明更新日期。

SilverMind Privacy Policy

1. About the App

SilverMind is a daily-life and cognitive practice app designed primarily for older adults. It offers short exercises involving everyday calculations, scheduling, memory, culture, and related topics.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

No account registration is required. Practice dates, answers, completion status, and feedback voluntarily entered by users may be stored locally on the device to display progress and history. Based on the current web application design, these records do not need to be sent to a developer-operated server for routine exercises.

If a user chooses to export a backup, the app creates a file that the user controls. Importing a backup reads a file selected by the user. Backup files may contain practice records or feedback and should be stored carefully.

3. Permissions, Connectivity and Third Parties

Core exercises are designed to work offline. The app does not require a name, phone number, or account registration. Actual permissions, embedded components, and network activity must be verified against the final released APK; this section should be updated if that review identifies additional processing.

4. Managing and Deleting Data

Where available, users may manage records through in-app features. Users may also clear the app's local data through Android Settings. Clearing app data is generally irreversible; please back up any records you wish to keep first. Uninstalling the app may also delete locally stored records.

5. Intended Audience

SilverMind is primarily intended for older adults and their families, not specifically for children. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal details, such as identification numbers or medical histories, in free-text feedback.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.
